Thermal Interface Temperature Testing

Thermal Interface Temperature Testing evaluates temperature conditions at the interface between contacting materials, surfaces or thermal interface materials.

The test can determine interface temperature, hot-side and cold-side temperature, temperature difference, heat flux, thermal resistance and thermal conductance under controlled conditions.

Key Parameters

Interface Temperature

Temperature measured directly or indirectly at the thermal interface.

Hot Temperature

Temperature of the heated side of the test assembly.

Cold Temperature

Temperature of the cooled side of the test assembly.

Temperature Difference

ΔT between the hot and cold sides or defined interface locations.

Heat Flux

Heat transferred through a defined interface area.

Thermal Resistance

Resistance calculated from temperature difference and heat flow.

Thermal Conductance

Ability of the interface to transfer thermal energy.

Contact Pressure

Pressure applied between contacting surfaces.

Interface Area

Effective area through which thermal energy is transferred.

Surface Roughness

Surface condition influencing thermal contact behavior.

Testing Methods

Steady-State Temperature Test

Temperature measurements after thermal equilibrium is reached.

Heat Flow Meter

Temperature and heat-flow measurement through a controlled specimen.

Guarded Hot Plate

Controlled thermal gradient and temperature measurement.

Thermal Test Vehicle

Integrated test assembly for interface thermal evaluation.

Comparative Method

Interface temperature is evaluated against a reference configuration.

Pressure-Controlled Test

Temperature behavior is measured at controlled contact pressure.

Transient Temperature Test

Temperature response is recorded during a changing thermal condition.

Results depend on material properties, interface geometry, contact pressure, heat flow, sensor placement, thermal gradients and environmental conditions.
